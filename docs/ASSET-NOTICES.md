# Asset and tooling notices

The repository's MIT license covers its original code and artwork. It does
not relicense third-party fonts, brand marks or imagery.

- Bundled, unmodified typefaces retain their SIL Open Font License notices
  under `folio/type/`. The profile uses IBM Plex Sans; other fonts belong to
  preserved design studies.
- Omarchy's official wordmark retains its original geometry. The derived
  orange version changes only its fill. The brand remains its owner's
  property and is not covered by this repository's MIT license.
- Discord and Ko-fi symbols come from their official brand assets. Source
  URLs and hashes are recorded in `folio/social-sources.json`. Transparent
  spacing and the Ko-fi PNG conversion do not imply ownership of those marks.
- Project and theme screenshots retain their original UI and wallpaper.
  Source URLs and hashes are recorded in `folio/provenance.json`,
  `folio/collection/sources.json`, `folio/popular-themes.json` and
  `assets/provenance.json`. Background rights and original credits remain
  with the linked projects; framing does not redraw the source content.
- The historical Rise image is a preserved V1 carousel capture, not a new
  V2 capture; its exact frame timestamp is unavailable. Historical Waybar
  captures show three real bar details.
- The local preview uses `github-markdown-css`, with its MIT notice retained
  at `scripts/preview/LICENSE-github-markdown-css`. Preview controls, local
  HTML, caches and browser review screenshots are not the GitHub profile.

The user-specified acknowledgements appear on the separate
[Acknowledgements](../ACKNOWLEDGEMENTS.md) page. These notices preserve the
asset/tooling context from the earlier acknowledgements document without
adding it to the compact public-facing list.
